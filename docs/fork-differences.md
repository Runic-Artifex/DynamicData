# Runic fork differences

Intentional differences from [reactivemarbles/DynamicData](https://github.com/reactivemarbles/DynamicData)
that must survive upstream merges. Keep IDs stable. Update an entry in the same
change that adds, changes or removes a difference; when upstream provides an
equivalent, remove the adaptation but keep its regression tests, and mark the
entry `retired`. Upstream status is as last reviewed; open gaps are tracked as
[fork issues](https://github.com/Runic-Artifex/DynamicData/issues).

| DD-040 | `ObservableChangeSet.Create` overloads whose task returns nothing (cache and list) complete when the task completes, fail when it fails, cancel it on unsubscribe and deliver its outcome on the completing thread; the list cancellable overload connects before the task runs, so changes arrive as they happen. | DD-041 | List `AutoRefreshOnObservable` (and `AutoRefresh`) follows the cache child policy: a re-evaluator error ends only that item's refresh subscription; the stream completes once the source has completed and every item subscription has ended. | Consistent list/cache behavior after list `MergeMany` started forwarding child errors (DD-028, fork issue #7). | Fork only; upstream list `AutoRefreshOnObservable` inherits list `MergeMany`'s child-error behavior (#1147). |
| DD-042 | Cache, list and virtualized `Switch` publish the previous source's removals with the new source's first non-empty changeset delivered during subscription as one changeset (cache: Remove, Update for re-added keys, then the new changes, without indexes; list: Clear, then the new changes). Delayed or empty sources still get the removals first; pending removals precede a terminal notification. List `Switch` has the same reentrant handoff loop and exclusive subscription ownership as the cache (#1188): a source selected while the previous one is subscribed or torn down starts afterwards, and the latest selection wins. | Subscribers saw an empty collection between sources (fork issue #8); list `Switch` lacked the cache's teardown-time reentrancy guard (fork issue #18). | Open issue [#1150](https://github.com/reactivemarbles/DynamicData/issues/1150). |
| DD-043 | List and cache `ExpireAfter` and `ToObservableChangeSet`, and list `Filter` with predicate state, deliver through delivery queues without holding their gates; source `ExpireAfter` publishes expired items after the source edit commits, not from inside it. Disposing stops further delivery without waiting for one in progress on another thread; so does disposing every other queue-backed operator (cache and list `AutoRefresh`, `FilterOnObservable`, `Switch`, `MergeMany`, `MergeChangeSets`, `Or`, `BufferInitial`, joins, grouping, sorting, paging and virtualisation, `Bind`, `SortAndBind` and property observation), List `DisposeMany` (and list `GroupOn`, which uses it), cache `OnItemRemoved` and `AsyncDisposeMany` hand the teardown of their remaining items to the delivering thread, which runs it once its delivery returns, so no item is torn down while a delivery may use it; `AsyncDisposeMany` tracks a changeset's items before delivering it. `Switch` no longer waits when subscribing to its sources fails. | Cross-instance deadlocks when observers write into each other, and when disposing while holding a lock the observer of an in-flight delivery needs (fork issues #16, [#23](https://github.com/Runic-Artifex/DynamicData/issues/23); follow-up to DD-016/DD-028). | Fork only. |
| DD-044 | Primitives flavour: internal replacements in the root `DynamicData` namespace take precedence over ReactiveUI.Primitives' extension methods for library call sites and follow the System.Reactive contract: `Scan` ([ScanExtensions.cs](../src/DynamicData/Internal/ScanExtensions.cs)), `Do`, `DistinctUntilChanged` and `CombineLatest` with a result selector deliver callback, comparer and selector exceptions as `OnError`; `SubscribeSafe(observer)` lets an exception thrown by the observer's `OnNext` propagate to the producer (ending that subscription) instead of converting it to `OnError`. Callback failures (for example `OnItemAdded`, `ForEachChange`, the `WhenChanged` selector) therefore reach `OnError`, and a subscriber's own exception is thrown to the editing caller, as in the System.Reactive flavour. In both flavours, operators that subscribe callbacks (`Filter` with a dynamic predicate, predicate state or reapply signal, `MergeChangeSets`, the dynamic `Group`, `ExpireAfter`, `OfType`, `AsyncDisposeMany`, `ToObservableChangeSet`, `TransformOnObservable`/`MergeManyChangeSets` and the other `CacheParentSubscription` operators, and the lock-free `AsObservableCache`) use [OperatorSubscription.cs](../src/DynamicData/Internal/OperatorSubscription.cs) instead of the static `PrimitivesLinqExtensions.SubscribeSafe(...)` (#25): an exception thrown by the operator's own logic (predicates, selectors, comparers, item disposal) is still delivered as `OnError`, while an exception thrown by the downstream subscriber is thrown to the producer and detaches that subscription, as System.Reactive's `Observable.Create` does. Primitives operators that dispose themselves before rethrowing a downstream exception (`Defer`, `Take`, `Skip`, `SkipWhile`, `StartWith`, `Using`, `Catch`) are kept: without the conversion they end the subscription like System.Reactive's subscription safeguard. The System.Reactive flavour keeps System.Reactive's operators. | Primitives 8.4/9.0 `Scan`, `Do`, `DistinctUntilChanged` and `CombineLatest` throw callback exceptions to the producer; `SubscribeSafe` converts observer exceptions to `OnError`, which an upstream `Defer`, `Take`, `Skip`, `DistinctUntilChanged` (or `Scan`) that had disposed itself before rethrowing then dropped. Failures in `Transform` factories, `Sum`/`Maximum` selectors and similar operators (#17), and in callbacks and subscribers downstream of most operators (#19), were lost, converted or thrown to the editor. The static `SubscribeSafe(source, onNext, ...)` delivered a subscriber's exception back to it as `OnError` (Primitives) or lost it (ReactiveUI.Primitives.Reactive), and `ToObservableChangeSet` and `OfType` caught it themselves (#25). | Fork only (Primitives flavour; the operator subscriptions of #25 in both flavours). |
| DD-045 | Queue-serialized operators with several inputs also deliver their output through their shared delivery queue: cache `Sort` with comparer changes or resorting, `Page`, `Virtualise`, `SortAndPage`, `SortAndVirtualize`, `Transform`/`TransformAsync` with forced transforms, `BatchIf`, `Group` and `GroupWithImmutableState` with a regrouper, and dynamic `Group`, `InnerJoin`, `LeftJoin`, `RightJoin`, `FullJoin`, dynamic `And`/`Or`/`Xor`/`Except`, `MergeManyChangeSets` (to cache or list changes, with or without a source comparer), `TransformOnObservable`, `GroupOnObservable`, `TransformManyAsync`, `TransformMany` with observable children, `QueryWhenChanged` with an item trigger and `TransformToTree`; list `Sort`, `Page`, `Virtualise`, dynamic `Filter`, `Filter` with predicate state, `BufferIf`, `AutoRefresh`, static and dynamic `And`/`Or`/`Xor`/`Except`, `MergeChangeSets`, `MergeMany`, `MergeManyChangeSets` (to list or cache changes), `TransformMany` with observable children and `GroupWithImmutableState` with a regrouper. A notification an observer causes through another input of the same operator, for example by writing into its source, is delivered after the observer returns instead of re-entrantly; cache `BatchIf` starts the next batch before publishing one. | The shared queue delivers a notification raised on its delivering thread inline, so such feedback reached the observer inside the delivery still running (an Rx grammar violation) and `BatchIf` published batched changes twice (fork issues [#24](https://github.com/Runic-Artifex/DynamicData/issues/24) and [#30](https://github.com/Runic-Artifex/DynamicData/issues/30), follow-up to DD-016). | Fork only; upstream's shared queue delivers same-thread re-entrant notifications inline. |
